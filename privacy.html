Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

This policy describes how the Private Calendar Assistant, a private and owner-operated productivity integration, handles Google Calendar data.

Information accessed

When authorized by the owner, the integration may access Google Calendar information needed for a requested task, including event titles, dates and times, descriptions, locations, attendees, conferencing information, calendar identifiers, and related event metadata.

How information is used

Calendar information is used only to perform tasks requested by the owner, such as preparing schedules, identifying conflicts, supplying meeting context, and generating private briefings. Calendar changes are not made unless the owner explicitly requests and approves the specific action.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in a private, access-controlled environment and are not included in this website or its source repository. Calendar information may appear in private assistant sessions or owner-requested briefing files. Those private records remain under the owner’s control and may be deleted by the owner.

Sharing and disclosure

Calendar information is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or made public. It may be processed by the technical service providers configured by the owner only as necessary to perform the requested functionality. The operator does not intentionally use Google Calendar data to train public or shared AI models.

Security

The integration uses Google OAuth authorization and keeps access credentials separate from this public informational site. Access is limited to the owner-operated environment. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, but reasonable access controls are used to protect credentials and private outputs.

Owner control and revocation

The owner may revoke the integration’s Google access at any time through the Google Account permissions page. Revocation prevents future API access. The owner may also remove locally retained credentials and private generated records.

Limited-use commitment

The integration’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

If the integration’s data practices materially change, this policy will be updated before the changed practices are used.

Contact

Questions may be directed to the developer contact shown on the integration’s Google OAuth consent screen.
